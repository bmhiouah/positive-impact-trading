Why “Positive Impact”

Four ethical-finance principles shape what the lab is allowed to buy.

No short selling

No profiting from others’ losses, no selling what you don’t own.

No interest, no leverage

Interest concentrates wealth and fuels inequality and debt-driven excess.

Good for society, low debt

Harmful sectors excluded; debt / market cap < 33%.

Real ownership, long-only

Own real businesses; risk is shared, not shifted.

How it works

From market data to a protected order, in eight steps.

1 · BROKER

IB Gateway

Single paper session via ib_insync

Capital is read from the account API, never configured.
2 · DATA

Data ingestion

  • Contract details
  • 1 year of daily bars
  • Delayed quotes
3 · STORE

SQLite store

One clean local database with idempotent upserts, per-symbol coverage and an audit log. marketmind.db
4 · ETHICS GATE

Impact compliance screen

Sector + debt-ratio gate. Whitelisted impact ETFs are exempt.
5 · STRATEGIES

Strategy catalogue

  • Trend: golden cross 50/200
  • Breakout: 20-day high on volume
  • RSI dip · 12-week momentum
  • Impact: debt / market cap < 33%
6 · SIGNAL
BUY
Strongest long-only signal per symbol. No SELL signals: exits belong to the bracket.
7 · EXECUTION

Bracket order

Entry + take-profit +5% + stop-loss −3%.
No naked orders: no reference price, no order.
8 · MONITOR

Journal & cockpit

Every bracket and one row per day, shown in a Streamlit cockpit. P&L split: trading vs interest.

Every entry is a protected bracket

Resting TP/SL stay at IBKR even if the algo stops.
